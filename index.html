Expert Project Manager and Physics Team Lead at Mercor AI. Physicist and mathematician working at the intersection of fundamental science and machine learning.

Previously, I completed a double Bachelor's in Physics and Mathematics at the University of Guilan, graduating summa cum laude and ranking first in both programs. Today, my work centers on training and evaluating frontier language models — from OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Anthropic, and Meta — on advanced reasoning in mathematics and physics.
